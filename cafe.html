At the Café

Ordering a drink and paying — the phrases you need over coffee.

Useful sentences

I'll have a large latte and a blueberry muffin, please.
我要一杯大杯拿铁和一个蓝莓松饼。
Could I get that to go, please?
能打包带走吗？
Would you like anything else with that?
还要点别的吗？
How much is it in total?
一共多少钱？
Can I pay by card?
可以刷卡吗？
Keep the change.
不用找了。

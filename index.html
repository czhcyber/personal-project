A home conceived for the night. Two pale volumes rest on a dark stone base, gathering glass, garden and warm light into a quiet mountain retreat.

A self-initiated architectural study about night, light and the rooms we keep after dark. Made for architecture-curious visitors.Designed & built by CZH · Fictional residence / 2026

SPACES

The dark rug and sofa base align with the floor, letting the living room unfold cleanly from the ground. The front-right view keeps the garden and fireplace in depth.

F1 · 3.2 m ceiling · X-RAY READY

A glass tea room cantilevered over the left garden, carried by four slender steel columns. A single warm point light holds the night temperature inside the Low-E glass box.

NEW WING · TRANSMISSION 1.0 · F2

A walnut dining table and a dark stone island form an open kitchen. The pendant lights are set low, keeping the night light on the table.

F1 · OPEN PLAN · 4 SEATS

The master suite faces the garden and the night. Bed, bench and reading chair sit inside one furniture silhouette; when selected, a wireframe outlines the room.

F2 · GLASS CORNER · 42 m²

A light study and guest room replaces the dark box: full-height glass faces the view, warm light leaks through the timber screen. The monitor opens NOCTURNE OS; the notes, material tray, massing model and archive box open the house’s project notes.

INTERACTIVE · ATELIER / OS / SHELF / LAMP

The garage sits against the rear-right of the house. A ramp descends from the ground, its edge lights connecting retaining wall, structural spine and white slab into one continuous detail.

SUB-LEVEL · RAMP · RETAINING WALL

A second-floor viewing terrace enclosed by glass, continuous with the study balcony. From here you can read the tea room, timber screen and sunken garage as one massing relationship.

F2 · GLASS RAIL · L-SHAPED VIEW

The onsen pavilion extends from the front-left of the villa. Stone, timber screen and warm water form the end of the night garden; steam and pool light keep their own temperature in rain or darkness.

GARDEN · ONSEN · STEAM / LIGHT

The rooms gather around a central void. By day the house opens to light; after dark, the interior light bands become part of the garden.

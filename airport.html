At the Airport

Check-in, security and boarding — the phrases you need before you fly.

Useful sentences

I'd like to check in for my flight to London, please.
我想办理飞往伦敦的登机手续。
Could I see your passport and boarding pass?
能看一下您的护照和登机牌吗？
How many bags are you checking in?
您要托运几件行李？
Please place your laptop in a separate tray.
请把笔记本电脑放在单独的托盘里。
The flight is delayed by thirty minutes.
航班延误了三十分钟。
What time does boarding start?
什么时候开始登机？

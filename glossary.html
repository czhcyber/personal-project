Quick reference

Glossary

The positions and words you will see on the board, in plain language.

Positions

CodePositionWhat they do
GKGoalkeeperThe last line of defence; the only player who may use their hands inside the box.
CBCentre-backStays central at the back, wins headers and blocks shots.
FB / LB / RBFull-backDefends the flanks and often pushes forward to cross the ball.
DMDefensive midfielderSits in front of the defence and breaks up attacks.
CMCentral midfielderLinks defence and attack, keeps the ball moving.
AMAttacking midfielderPlays behind the striker, creates chances and shoots.
LW / RWWingerWide attacker who runs at defenders and crosses.
ST / CFStrikerThe main goalscorer, playing furthest forward.

Tactical terms

TermMeaning
PressingClosing the opponent down quickly to win the ball high up the pitch.
Counter-attackBreaking forward fast as soon as the ball is won.
OverlapA player runs around the outside of a teammate to offer a wide pass.
Through ballA pass played into space behind the defence for a runner.
OffsideAn attacker is behind the last defender when the ball is played.

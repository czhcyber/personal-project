Registration, classes and the library — the phrases you need as a student.

Useful sentences

Where can I register for the new semester?
新学期在哪里注册？
The lecture starts at nine and ends at half past ten.
讲座九点开始，十点半结束。
Could you tell me how to borrow books from the library?
能告诉我怎么从图书馆借书吗？
I need to hand in my assignment before Friday.
我需要在周五之前交作业。
The exam has been postponed to next Monday.
考试推迟到了下周一。
Is the canteen open on weekends?
食堂周末开门吗？
